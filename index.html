🤟 SignBridge

Hold a sign steady for about a second until the green bar fills up. To send the same sign again, drop your hand first. Wave an open hand side to side to say goodbye. You can edit any phrase.

Tips for better captions
• Use headphones so your mic doesn't pick up other people's voices.
• Each person captions their own voice, which is far more accurate than captioning sound from the speakers.
• Works best in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
